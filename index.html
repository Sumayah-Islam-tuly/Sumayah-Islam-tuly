My Journey

From Freelancer
to Founder

I started as a freelancer with big dreams and a lot of self-doubt. Through real client projects, I learned that success in web development goes far beyond code — it's about mindset, consistency, and the communities we build around us.

I built my expertise in WordPress and Shopify, founded Xixify to help businesses grow online, and became an active contributor in WordPress communities worldwide. Today, I speak at events to inspire other freelancers to achieve sustainable success.

Success is not only about technical skills. Mindset, consistency, and community support are equally important.

What I Believe

Personal
Philosophy

Consistency Beats Talent

Show up every day, do the work, and results follow. Talent opens doors, but consistency keeps them open.

Community Creates Opportunities

The WordPress community taught me that helping others is the fastest way to grow yourself.

Learning Never Stops

Technology evolves daily. Staying curious and adaptable is the key to lasting relevance.

Success Is A Long-Term Game

Quick wins fade. Building something meaningful takes patience, grit, and vision.

Mental Health Matters

Freelancing can be isolating. Taking care of your mind is as important as taking care of your code.
